- **AeroAegis — Python Developer ("remote internship"):** despite the internship framing, the JD requires 1–1.5 years of professional Python backend experience.
- **Weekday (YC W21) — Software Engineer Intern:** the JD opens with "This role is for one of our clients" — a staffing/recruiting marketplace posting, not a direct employer.
- **Scalenut — AI Video Intern:** despite the "AI" branding, the actual work is video editing/UGC ad creative production — no coding or backend relevance.
- **ReasonCore AI — AI Data Intern:** subject-matter data-authoring/QA for LLM training sets, not an engineering role.
- A cluster of templated "Data/Machine Learning Intern | Entry Level | Fresher | ... | Remote" postings from Crossing Infotech, Dexter's Tech, Vortenza Systems, Unified Mentor, Devryxa, Nexal IIT, internmo, and F6IT — all with suspiciously round low applicant counts (~25) and identical formatting, reading as mass-produced staffing/training-scheme spam rather than real single postings.
- Standard noise dropped without much debate: full-time non-intern roles LinkedIn's AI search surfaced anyway (Accenture "AI/ML Computational Science Associate" x3, Amazon "Applied Scientist I" x3, Infosys, Conga, PRI India, Optum, Ionic Wealth), a duplicate generic "AI Agent Observability Engineer @ Jobgether" listing (kept only the Binance-branded version of the same role), and a PhD-only Google internship.

**Time-sensitive:** Google's Application Engineering Intern postings (Summer 2027 and Winter 2027 cohorts) both state applications must be submitted **no later than Sept 15, 2026 — i.e. today.** If this is of interest, apply before end of day.

**Known gap:** the "Remote, international/foreign company" sub-bucket surfaced only one strong candidate this run (Binance, via the Jobgether recruiting platform) — most AI internships at foreign companies found in the raw results were onsite outside India with no remote option, so they were excluded rather than misrepresented as remote-eligible.

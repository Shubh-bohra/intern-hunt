## Method & data quality note

Six scraper runs were completed before this account hit Apify's **monthly usage hard limit**, which stopped two further planned searches (additional Backend/Data Engineer titles in Bengaluru, and a broader remote AI/ML sweep). Everything below comes from the runs that did complete — no listings were fabricated or filled in from memory.

Across ~120 raw scraped postings, the large majority were **excluded** as noise:
- A cluster of near-identical mass postings from the same handful of accounts (`Unified Mentor Private Limited` / `Unified Mentor Jobs` / `WebBoost Solutions by UM` / `UM IT Private Limited`, plus `Zenithbyte`, `Devryxa`, `Vortenza Systems`) spamming dozens of interchangeable "PYTHON DEVELOPMENT INTERN" / "CYBER SECURITY INTERN" / "FRONTEND DEVELOPMENT INTERN" titles with no real company presence — classic training-mill/spam pattern.
- Non-technical roles mistagged as tech (HR, fundraising, placement, business development, digital marketing interns).
- Roles requiring a PhD (Apple, Google) or 6+ years' experience despite an "intern"-adjacent tag (a recruiting-agency-posted "Senior Gen AI Engineer" from HuntingCube/MantriAI, clearly a staffing agency, requiring 6+ years — excluded on both counts).
- Generic IT-services/staffing-flavored listings (TalentXM, Codefeast) with no distinguishing engineering substance.

What survived that filter is a **short, high-confidence list** rather than a padded one, per the run's instructions.

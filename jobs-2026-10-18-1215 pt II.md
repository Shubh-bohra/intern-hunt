## Notes & Caveats

1. **Verify all listings before applying.** Several listings are from aggregators (jobfound.org, cloudsoftsol.com, freshershunt.in) that repost or summarize roles. Always confirm the role is live and the details are correct on the company's own careers portal.
2. **Sarvam AI careers page** should be checked directly — both the Backend Intern and the Backend Engineer Samvaad roles may have different statuses; given the direct resume match (Sarvam AI's own STT API was used in the Tez Health work), this is an exceptional application target.
3. **SuperKalam voice-first role** is the highest-priority application: the JD is an almost-literal match to the Tez Health project. Email a Loom demo as the CTO suggests.
4. **International remote roles** (truly global/worldwide, not India-only remote): No verified international remote fresher-eligible AI roles were found this run. RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, and Remotive did not return relevant fresher listings in October 2026.
5. **Eligibility for 2027 batch:** Several "fresher" drives specify 2024–2026 pass-outs. Shubh is on a 2023–2027 batch (graduating 2027). Clarify batch eligibility before applying to drives. Internships are not affected by this.

- The scraper's `contractType` field was inconsistent for several listings tagged "Intern" in the title (e.g., Sprinto, Automation Anywhere, FiduraAI showed `Full-time` rather than `Internship`) — flagged individually above rather than silently trusted.
- `applyUrl` was empty for every scraped item; the LinkedIn job-view URL (`url` field) is used as the application link and is the correct entry point to apply.
- Excluded as staffing/generic/low-quality: PwC Acceleration Center India ("Backend Developer – Intern, Specialist"), MediNex Workforce, Course Bhai (Jabil reseller/lead-gen listing, unpaid "Volunteer" contract type), Fresher Door (third-party aggregator repost of a Riverbed drive), WEBBOOST Solution IT Services.
